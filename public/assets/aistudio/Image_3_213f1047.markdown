## Brand & Style
This design system embraces modern Google Material Design 3 (M3) Web aesthetics, combining utilitarian clarity, expansive informational density, and welcoming human-centric geometry. 

Targeted at daily knowledge workers, information seekers, and web application users, the interface evokes focus, high credibility, and effortless accessibility. The aesthetic centers on:
- High-clarity white and light-neutral surfaces punctuated by purposeful brand accents.
- Soft container fills that provide immediate category recognition and cognitive ease.
- Precise, tactile interaction points grounded in standard Google ergonomics: pill controls, hairline borders, and fluid tonal feedback.

## Colors
The palette leverages Google's core chromatic spectrum anchored by a restrained light surface hierarchy.

- **Primary (`#1A73E8`) & Deep Blue (`#0B57D0`)**: Used for focal actions, active selection states, navigation indicator lines, and primary callouts.
- **Surface Foundations**: Canvas background rests on `#F8F9FA`, creating subtle contrast against elevated pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards and header bars.
- **Semantic Accent Containers**: Soft tints (`#E8F0FE`, `#FCE8E6`, `#E6F4EA`, `#FEF7E0`) designate state changes, categorization chips, and callout badges with low visual fatigue.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Structured strictly with hairline gray (`#DADCE0`) for crisp boundary separation without heavy visual weight.
- **Text & Glyphs**: Primary reading color is `#1F1F1F` for maximum legibility; supporting metadata and auxiliary icons default to `#444746` and `#5F6368`.

## Typography
Typography channels the clarity of Google Sans and standard Google web properties using **Plus Jakarta Sans** for rounded, friendly editorial headlines, and **Roboto Flex** for utilitarian body copy and compact UI tokens.

- Display headers use medium (`500`) weights rather than heavy bolds to maintain a refined, professional publication posture.
- Body copy is spaced for rapid vertical scanning, with optical letter-spacing calibrated for dense informational feeds.
- Labels, tabs, and interactive chip controls use medium weight (`500`) with intentional tracking for distinct legibility at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive responsive structure aligned with Material 3 responsive grid guidance:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 24px gutters, max-width bounded to 1280px or 1440px for editorial feeds. App top bars stretch edge-to-edge, maintaining 32px safe outer padding.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 16px to 24px gutters and 24px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 600px)**: 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins. Top search collapses from persistent pill to sticky icon/pill overlay.
- Multi-feed layouts utilize an asymmetrical distribution (e.g., primary feed 8 columns, contextual sidebar 4 columns).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation favors crisp boundary containment and subtle tonal contrast over dramatic drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering**: The primary canvas rests at Level 0 (`#F8F9FA`). Content cards rest on Level 1 (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid `#DADCE0` perimeter border.
- **Top Bar & App Header**: Fixed top navigation sits at `#FFFFFF` with a bottom hairline border (`1px solid #DADCE0`). On scroll, introduce a subtle 4px blur ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.08), 0 4px 8px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.04)`.
- **Search Bar**: Rest at Level 0 tonal contrast (`#F1F3F4` fill, borderless) transitioning on focus to Level 2 (`#FFFFFF` with `0 1px 6px rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.28)` and transparent border).
- **Floating Controls & Modals**: Dropdown menus, tooltips, and dialogs utilize M3 elevation (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.3), 0 4px 8px 3px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.15)`).

## Shapes
Geometry emphasizes dual curvature scales:

- **Cards & Content Containers**: Base geometry uses 16px (`rounded-xl` / 1rem to 1.5rem equivalent) to create friendly, distinct informational blocks.
- **Inputs & Action Pills**: Search bars, interactive action buttons, and category chips utilize full pill roundedness (`9999px` / 100vh radius).
- **Tooltips & Menus**: Internal menus and utility popovers use 8px (`rounded-md`) corners.

## Components

### Top Navigation & Search
- **App Bar**: Clean `#FFFFFF` background, 64px height, bottom bordered with `#DADCE0`.
- **Search Bar**: Pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 9999px`), 48px height, `#F1F3F4` resting background, no initial border. Leading search magnifying glass (`#5F6368`), centered placeholder text, trailing action icons. Expands cleanly on focus with white background and elevated drop shadow.

### Buttons & Chips
- **Primary Pill Button**: High-emphasis `#1A73E8` fill, white `#FFFFFF` label text, 36px to 40px height, full pill radius. Hover: `#1557B0`. Active ripple state.
- **Tonal Button**: Container fill `#E8F0FE`, text `#1A73E8`, full pill radius.
- **Filter / M3 Chips**: 32px height, fully rounded, bordered with `#DADCE0` on `#FFFFFF` base. Selected state swaps background to `#E8F0FE` and text/icon to `#0B57D0` with zero outline.

### Navigation Tabs
- Top level category tabs feature `#1F1F1F` text (500 weight), transparent padding, and an active indicator marked by a **3px thick Google Blue (`#1A73E8`) bar** directly anchored to the bottom divider.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **News / Content Card**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` border-radius, hairline stroke `1px solid #DADCE0`. Hover state triggers a subtle elevation shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(60,64,67,0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(60,64,67,0.08)` without altering border geometry.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Outlined style with 4px border radius or pill style depending on context, resting `#DADCE0` outline, active state `#1A73E8` 2px border.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Primary color `#1A73E8` when active, `#5F6368` outline when unselected. High-contrast white check indicator.
